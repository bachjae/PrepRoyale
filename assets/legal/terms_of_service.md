## Battle Mode and Competitive Features

### Battle Rules

**Fair Play:**
All battles must be conducted fairly:
- No external assistance during timed questions
- No communication with opponents during battles
- No exploitation of bugs or glitches
- Answer within the 30-second time limit
- One answer per question (cannot change answers)

**Health and Damage System:**
- Players start with 100 health points
- Correct answers deal damage to opponents (10-15 HP based on speed)
- Wrong answers deal self-damage (8 HP)
- Timeouts deal self-damage (15 HP)
- Battle ends when a player's health reaches 0 or all 20 questions are answered

**Battle Abandonment:**
- Leaving a battle in progress counts as a forfeit
- Forfeits are recorded as losses
- Repeated forfeiting may result in account restrictions
- Battles inactive for more than 1 hour are automatically abandoned

**Battle Results:**
- All battle results are final
- Results are calculated server-side and cannot be disputed
- Wins and losses are permanently recorded
- Statistics affect leaderboard rankings

### Matchmaking

**Matchmaking Criteria:**
- Players matched within ±3 levels
- Same test type (SAT or ACT)

- Rate limited to 20 attempts per 5 minutes

**Fair Matchmaking:**

- You may cancel matchmaking at any time before a match is found
